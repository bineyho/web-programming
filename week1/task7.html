Chocolate Pancakes

What You Need

  • 1 cup flour
  • 2 tablespoons cocoa powder
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder

Steps

  1. Mix the flour, cocoa powder, sugar, and baking powder in a bowl.
  2. Add the egg and milk, then mix until the batter is smooth.
  3. Heat a pan and add a little butter.
  4. Be careful when touching the hot pan to avoid getting burned.
  5. Pour some batter into the pan and cook until bubbles appear.
  6. Flip the pancake and cook the other side until golden brown.
  7. Enjoy the pancakes while they are warm!
